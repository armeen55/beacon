# Connector Automation Map

**How automated is Beacon once everything is connected?**

Once all six sources are connected, Beacon runs the entire find -> fix -> rank -> prove loop on autopilot every night. It reads all of your data, drafts the exact fixes, and ranks them by real-world impact. The **one** thing it never does without you is push a change to your live website: **every Wix publish is a single one-click approval, and there is no zero-touch auto-publish.** Five of the six sources are strictly read-only by design (Beacon literally cannot change anything in Google, your analytics, SEMrush, the AI-tracking tool, or Clarity). Only Wix has a write path, and it is gated behind your explicit approval, a per-change snapshot/undo, and a hard cap of 10 publishes per site per day.

This document is written so a non-technical owner can understand every line. No jargon. The AI-answer source is called **"AI answer tracking"** — never a vendor name — in anything a customer sees.

## What this means in plain English

- **Five of the six sources Beacon only reads.** It physically cannot change anything in Google Search Console, your Google Analytics, SEMrush, your AI answer tracking, or Microsoft Clarity. Every "fix" Beacon proposes from that data is published through **Wix**, and only after you approve it.
- **Beacon does the thinking on autopilot.** Every night it pulls all your data, finds the weak spots, drafts the exact fix, and ranks the list by what actually matters (pages that make money, search terms with real demand, friction on pages that convert).
- **You stay in control of your live site.** Beacon never auto-publishes. Each change is a separate one-click "Approve & Push," it saves an undo point first, and it won't publish more than 10 changes a day. New blog posts, images, and any URL/menu/page-deletion changes are always done by you in Wix.

## Cross-source automations (only possible when 2+ sources are connected)

1. **GSC demand x AI-answer gap -> which answer block to write first.** When AI answer tracking shows a competitor (not you) being recommended on a topic AND Google Search Console shows real people searching that topic, Beacon writes the answer block for the highest-demand losing topic first — so your effort lands where customers are actually looking.
2. **GA4 money x AI/GSC opportunity -> rank by dollars, not just clicks.** GA4 tells Beacon which pages turn visitors into customers; Beacon multiplies each search/AI opportunity by that proven value, so a fix on a page that books jobs outranks the same fix on a page that doesn't. *(Today this uses conversion count; adding GA4 revenue would make it follow real dollars.)*
3. **SEMrush near-miss x GSC confirmation -> rewrite the title that's one step from page one.** SEMrush flags a term you rank #4-20 for that isn't in your title, GSC's own data confirms the near-miss, and Beacon writes the new title — and on Wix, publishes it on your approval.
4. **SEMrush keyword-gap x AI-answer gap -> one new page that wins both Google and AI.** When a competitor owns a term you don't rank for AND the AI assistants cite that competitor on the matching topic, Beacon proposes a single new page that closes both gaps.
5. **Clarity friction x GA4 value -> fix the leaky page that actually matters.** Clarity finds the page where visitors rage-click or hit errors; GA4 says that page also converts — Beacon prioritizes the leak that's quietly costing you customers.
6. **Wix publish x GSC re-crawl + GA4/AI proof -> close the loop and prove it worked.** After you approve a Wix change, Beacon confirms it went live, then watches GSC rankings, GA4 traffic, and AI-answer citations on that page to prove the edit moved the needle. *(A "request Google re-crawl" step — see gaps — would speed this up.)*

## Owner checklist to reach maximum automation

- [ ] **Google Search Console** — Click Connect once, approve Google's read-only access. Your site must already be verified in GSC. Reconnect only if you revoke access.
- [ ] **Google Analytics 4** — Sign in with the Google account that owns your Analytics; pick your property from the dropdown (no data flows until you do). Mark your important events (calls, forms, purchases) as "key events" inside GA4. Reconnect if idle past 7 days.
- [ ] **SEMrush** — Be on a plan with API access (the Business plan; Pro/Guru/Starter don't include it); add a balance of API "units" (they don't roll over — keep it funded); paste your API key into Beacon once. Optional: create a Position Tracking project for daily AI-Overview tracking.
- [ ] **AI answer tracking** — Be on the provider's top (Enterprise) plan; email their support to turn API access on; mint one key with an expiration date and paste it into Beacon; set up at least one topic; enable the Agent Analytics add-on for AI-crawler and AI-referral data. Re-paste a fresh key when it expires.
- [ ] **Microsoft Clarity** — Have Clarity installed and collecting sessions; as a project admin, go to Settings -> Data Export -> Generate new API token -> paste it into Beacon. Rotate the token if a teammate with access leaves.
- [ ] **Wix** — Paste a Wix site API key + your Site ID; tell Beacon which collection holds your pages, the slug field, the URL prefix, and the title/heading/meta field (until mapped, those edits stay copy-paste-ready). Paid Wix plan required; Stores app for product-page schema.
- [ ] **Ongoing** — Approve each Wix change with one click (there's no auto-publish); keep keys/tokens current; keep your SEMrush unit balance funded; handle new blog posts, images, and any URL/menu/page-deletion changes yourself in Wix.

## The honest autopilot framing

Beacon is **continuous**: it finds the opportunities, drafts the fixes, ranks them by real impact, and proves whether they worked — every night, across all six sources, without you watching.

But Beacon is **not zero-touch publishing.** The hand-off point is deliberate and lives at exactly one place — your live website:

- **You one-click approve.** Every change to your live Wix site is a separate, explicit "Approve & Push." Beacon saves an undo point first and won't exceed 10 publishes a day.
- **No silent auto-publish, ever.** Beacon never changes your live site on its own. It cannot change anything at all in Google, Analytics, SEMrush, AI answer tracking, or Clarity — those are read-only by design.
- **Some things are always yours.** New blog posts, images, and any URL, menu, or page-deletion changes are handled by you in Wix.

In one line: **Beacon does the watching, the thinking, and the writing on autopilot — you do the approving.**
