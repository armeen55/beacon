## Key patterns

- **Everyone gates on prompt volume + # of platforms.** The Profound $499 "Lite" tracks only ChatGPT with 50 prompts — it's a lead magnet, not the real product.
- **True mid-tier ACV is $199–$499.** Hall Business ($499), Otterly Pro ($989), Peec Advanced (~$465), AthenaHQ self-serve ($295), Scrunch Core ($250) all cluster here.
- **Enterprise is $1.5k–$5k+**. That's where real feature parity lives (API, SSO, unlimited seats, SOC2).
- **No one is vertical.** Every tool is horizontal. Zero tools position for local construction / design / trades. This is Beacon's moat alongside the change-log attribution wedge.
- **Free audit / trial is table stakes.** Profound, Hall, Peec, Otterly, Scrunch all offer one. AthenaHQ and Evertune don't — and both get dinged for it in reviews.

## Pricing recommendation for Beacon

1. **Anchor at $499/mo Pro** (your ACV target) — ~100 prompts, 5 platforms (ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/AIO/Gemini), weekly change-log + causal attribution, 1 location/firm. This matches Hall Business and undercuts Profound Growth on real coverage.
2. **Add a $249/mo Starter** — 40 prompts, 3 platforms, monthly cadence. Converts from free audit; lets a $5M firm say yes without procurement.
3. **Add a $1,499/mo Agency/Multi-Location** — 5 firms or locations, white-label read-only share link, API. Matches Hall Enterprise floor; lower than Profound Enterprise.
4. **Offer a free "AI Visibility Audit"** (one-time prompt run + top 3 fixes + competitor snapshot). This is the proven top-of-funnel in this category — every paid competitor uses it.
5. **Do NOT compete on prompt volume.** Pitch causal change-log attribution ("we tell you which content edit moved citations, not just that citations moved") — no one in this table can replicate that, and it justifies the $499 price without a 1,000-prompt race.
